A show about making friends in a new city.

The Concept

Two Newbies.
One Couch.

…and a whole city of strangers.

We just moved to Houston. Every episode, we invite someone new (or old) to town over. We play games, eat weird snacks, swap a few secrets, then head out to meet neighbors in the community. By the credits, we've made a new friend, and started a new micro‑community.
